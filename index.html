Mappers Unbound is a student-led organization dedicated to participating in the Missing Maps Project and other map-related activities.

About Mappers Unbound


Mappers Unbound exists to create a positive impact on the community. For instance, the Missing Maps Project enables NGOs to locate vulnerable populations by viewing mapped buildings, streets, and waterways. Additionally, Mappers Unbound aims to lead projects and activities such as creating maps for proper chemical disposal, minimizing the harmful effects of chemicals and pharmaceuticals on our surrounding environment.
