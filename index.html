Mappers Unbound is a student-led organization dedicated to participate to Missing Map Project and other map related activities.

About Mappers Unbound


Mappers Unbound exists to create positive impact on community. For instance, Missing Map Project enables NGOs to find where people are populated by seeing the mapped buildings,streets, and waterways. Also, Mappers Unbound is aiming for project and other activities such as creating map for proper chemical dispose to minimize chemicals/pharmaceutical's harmful effect on the environment around the us.
